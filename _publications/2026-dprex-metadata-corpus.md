## Abstract

The DP-R&#124;EX Metadata Corpus is a manually curated study-level dataset providing a structured overview of empirical research on racism, right-wing extremism, and adjacent research areas in German-speaking countries. It comprises metadata from 399 studies conducted between 1902 and 2025, drawn from the GESIS, DeZIM.fdz, and Qualiservice research data centres and a dedicated thematic collection from additional data providers. The corpus provides domain-specific metadata on theoretical concepts, social and minority groups, and data types, all of which are based on controlled vocabularies developed within the DP-R&#124;EX project. Complementing generic DDI Codebook study documentation, it includes harmonised information on sampling strategies, temporal research designs, modes of data collection, and fieldwork periods. Concepts and groups are represented as binary study-by-category indicators, enabling analyses of their frequency, temporal distribution, and co-occurrence. The controlled vocabularies are openly available through CESSDA, and the indexed studies are discoverable through the DP-R&#124;EX Data Explorer. The accompanying data manual documents variable coding, harmonisation, and mappings between original controlled-vocabulary terms and processed categories. As the corpus is restricted to well-documented and reusable research data, it represents the FAIR-aligned research landscape rather than an exhaustive inventory of empirical studies conducted in the research fields.
